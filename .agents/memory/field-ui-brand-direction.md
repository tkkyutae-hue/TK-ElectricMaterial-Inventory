---
name: Field UI brand direction
description: Visual rules for TK Electric Field Mode entry and mode-selection headers.
---

Use the TK Electric Field Mode palette for entry and mode-selection headers: deep green surfaces, a bright green accent, and a small outlined TK lockup. Keep preferences and sign-out controls together in a restrained bordered group rather than scattering them across the header.

**Why:** The selected brand-forward direction makes the app identity visible immediately while preserving a practical, uncluttered control hierarchy.

**How to apply:** Carry this treatment across login, account-request, and mode-selection entry points. At narrow widths, preserve controls first and hide nonessential brand subtext rather than allowing wrapping or overflow.
