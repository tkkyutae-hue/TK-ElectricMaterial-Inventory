PART 2 — MERGE PHOTO CELLS PER SUBGROUP BLOCK
For each lower subgroup/base-item block:

For each subgroup block:

Only merge the PHOTO column.

PART 5 — VISUAL STYLE
- keep current export grouping hierarchy

- keep the export readable and professional

PART 6 — SAFE IMAGE HANDLING
- if image fetch fails, leave the merged PHOTO cell blank and continue
